About Verity

Verity is a browser that treats every outbound request as something that has to be justified. It ships with one opinion: you should have to opt out of protection, never in.

The name, and the face

This browser is named after Verity, the smiling yellow sphere of the Minecraft horror series that became a meme — a friendly assistant that turns out to know a little too much. The joke maps onto a browser almost too well, so the whole interface is built around it: a bright, blocky, game-like surface over a very dark room.

The mascot on this page is original artwork drawn for this project in that character's visual language. Verity the character belongs to ThatMob's series, and none of its art is licensed for reuse, so this browser ships its own drawing rather than someone else's image.
